# Bus gallery photo credits

The coach photos in this folder are **representative fleet photos** used by the
"Bus Gallery" panel. Each operator in `pricing.js` maps two of them through the
`photos[]` array. All files come from Wikimedia Commons and are reused here under
their respective free licences (CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain).
